KinFlash Support

Help with family trees, flashcards, GEDCOM files, and TestFlight builds.

Getting Help

Email bill@1041soft.com with the app name, your device, OS version, and what you were trying to do.

Common Questions

Where is my family tree kept? KinFlash is designed around local family files. If you save or share a file through iCloud Drive, Files, Messages, Mail, or another Apple service, that service handles the transfer.

Can I bring in a tree from another genealogy tool? Yes. KinFlash is built to import and export GEDCOM so your family tree remains portable.

Why do flashcards ask about distant relatives? Cards are generated from the people and relationships in the tree you opened. Start with closer family first if you want a smaller practice deck.

How do I request TestFlight access? KinFlash currently has a public TestFlight link: join the beta here.
