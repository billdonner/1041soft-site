KinFlash

Build your family tree, learn who's who, and keep the file portable.

KinFlash is a commercial App Store release-track app for family-tree authoring and relationship practice. Build a tree, add relatives and details, then turn those relationships into flashcards that help the names and connections stick.

KinFlash imports and exports GEDCOM, so a tree can move between KinFlash and other genealogy tools instead of being trapped in one app. The app is designed around local ownership of the family file first.

AI-guided interview features are being developed for supported devices, with a focus on helping capture names, dates, places, and relationships from a conversation without taking control away from the person editing the tree.
