SMS Terms and Conditions

These terms govern the 1041Soft member scheduling SMS program.

Program

1041Soft sends operational scheduling messages to members of private recreational collectives operated through 1041Soft. Messages may include playing schedules, game reminders, and court-booking-window notices. Message frequency varies, typically about one weekly schedule plus relevant reminders.

Consent

Members opt in by providing their mobile number directly to an organizer when joining a collective, in person or by replying to an invitation. Consent is for scheduling messages only. No purchased or imported lists are used.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out immediately and permanently until you ask to be re-added. Reply HELP for help, or email bill@1041soft.com. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contact

Questions about this program may be sent to bill@1041soft.com. 1041Soft may suspend messaging when needed to protect members or comply with applicable requirements.
