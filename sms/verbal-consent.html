Verbal consent for SMS scheduling

This page documents the exact consent process used when an organizer enrolls an invited member.

Organizer script

“1041Soft operates the scheduling messages for this private recreational pickleball collective. If you provide your mobile number, 1041Soft will text you your playing schedule, game reminders, and occasional court-booking notices for this collective. This is operational information, not marketing. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop all messages, or HELP for assistance. Do you agree to receive these scheduling messages at this mobile number?”

Member consent

The organizer asks the invited member to confirm verbally. The member answers, “Yes.” The organizer then records the member’s name, mobile number, selected collective, date, organizer, and the fact that the member agreed to receive 1041Soft scheduling messages.

Confirmation

The organizer repeats the number back to the member and confirms that the member may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP or by telling the organizer. The member is not added from a purchased, imported, or third-party list.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to stop all messages immediately and permanently until the member asks to be re-added. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact bill@1041soft.com. Message and data rates may apply.
