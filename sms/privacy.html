SMS Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026.

Information used

For the member scheduling program, 1041Soft may store a member's name, mobile number, selected collective, scheduling preferences, consent record, and opt-out status. We use this information to send schedules, reminders, and to honor STOP and HELP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell mobile numbers or share them with third parties for marketing. We use a contracted messaging provider to transmit requested texts and process delivery and opt-out events. The provider receives the information needed to deliver the message and operate the messaging service.

Retention and choices

We retain messaging records only as needed to operate the collectives, document consent and opt-out requests, resolve delivery issues, and meet legal obligations. Reply STOP to stop messages. For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions, email bill@1041soft.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the program changes. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
