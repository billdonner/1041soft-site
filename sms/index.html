SMS member scheduling

Operational text messages for members of private recreational collectives operated through 1041Soft.

How enrollment works

Membership is by personal invitation. An organizer invites a prospective member to join a 1041Soft-operated collective. The prospective member provides a mobile number directly to the organizer, either in person or by replying to the invitation, for the express purpose of receiving that collective's playing schedule. Providing the number is the member's request to receive scheduling texts.

There is no purchased, imported, or third-party list. Numbers are used only for the member's selected collective and are not sold or shared for marketing.

What members receive

Messages may include weekly playing schedules, evening-before game reminders, and occasional notices that a court-booking window has opened. Frequency is usually about one weekly schedule plus relevant reminders. This program is operational only and contains no advertising, promotions, or third-party content.

Opt out or get help

Reply STOP to stop all messages immediately. A member may also tell the organizer, who will remove the number from the messaging list. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact bill@1041soft.com. Message and data rates may apply.
