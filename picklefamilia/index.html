PickleFamilia

Fair courts and a free club

The PickleFamilia app for iPhone and iPad is PickleFamilia Companion, free on the App Store. No account, and it's free, forever.

On Court

Anyone can run a game. Pick who showed up and the Companion builds fair pairings: nobody partners twice until everyone has, and extras rotate. Rounds, a timer and scores are all on your phone, and players tap Listen to see their own court live, over the Wi‑Fi in the room.

Your club, free

Your roster is your club. Name it, add your players, and share it with a link so your other organizers keep the same list. Names and ratings only, never phone numbers. Free forever.
