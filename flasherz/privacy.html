Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

Overview

Flasherz ("the App") is an educational flashcard app for children. Your family's privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks
  • We do not use cookies

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Family profiles: Names, age band selections, deck preferences, and speech settings that you configure. This data never leaves your device.
  • App preferences: Settings such as selected voice, speech enabled/disabled, and onboarding state. Stored via standard iOS mechanisms (UserDefaults).

This data is not transmitted to us or any third party. You can delete it at any time by removing the App from your device.

Network Requests

The App makes network requests to our server to fetch flashcard deck content (topics, questions, and answers). These requests:

  • Do not include any personal or device-identifying information
  • Contain only standard HTTP headers
  • Are used solely to retrieve educational content

If the server is unavailable, the App automatically uses built-in sample decks with no network activity.

Text-to-Speech

The App uses Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer to read content aloud. All speech processing occurs entirely on your device. No audio data is recorded or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Flasherz is designed for use by children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social media frameworks.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through an App update.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our Support page.
