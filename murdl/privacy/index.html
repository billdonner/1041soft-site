MURDL 16 Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

MURDL 16 collects nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, and no advertising. The developer never receives or sees any of your data.

What stays on your devices

Your game records, scores, and settings (board count, mode, layout, keyboard window position) are stored in the app's own container on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Your score book also syncs through your own iCloud account to your other devices signed into it; Apple stores that copy, and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app removes the local copy; Clear in Scores removes the records everywhere.

Challenges

When you send a friend a challenge, MURDL builds a link that carries the game's answers, the name you chose, and your result (boards solved, guesses, and time). The link goes only where you send it, through Messages, Mail, or whatever you pick. When your friend sends a result back, their link carries their name and result the same way. Challenges you send and receive are kept in your score book. The challenge page on 1041soft.com only shows a button that opens the link in MURDL; nothing in the link is sent to or stored on that site.

Purchases

MURDL Multiplayer is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from it.

No tracking

MURDL 16 does not use third-party SDKs, does not fingerprint your device, and does not read anything outside its own container.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and contains no content directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.
