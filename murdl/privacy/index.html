MURDL 16 Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

MURDL 16 collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. The app never connects to the network.

What stays on your Mac

Your game records, scores, and settings (board count, mode, layout, keyboard window position) are stored locally in the app's own sandboxed container on your Mac. They are not transmitted anywhere, and the developer cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

No tracking

MURDL 16 does not use third-party SDKs, does not fingerprint your device, and does not read anything outside its own container.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and contains no content, links, or purchases directed at children or anyone else. It collects no information from anyone.
