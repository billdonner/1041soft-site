Oenora Support

A private record of the wine you own, built from photographs of the labels.

What Oenora does

Point the camera, it works out the rest. Photograph a bottle and Oenora identifies it for your confirmation. Photograph a whole shelf of sealed wooden cases and it detects each case, reads the stencils, and offers one record per case — sizes included. Each wine gets an automatic consensus writeup, researched in the background from reputable sources.

Publish your cellar as a catalog. Shopkeeper mode lists the bottles you choose under your own shop's name and collects indications of interest from buyers. Oenora never processes payments or transactions — follow-up happens in Messages or Mail.

Talk to your cellar from the Mac. Connect an AI assistant such as Claude on the same machine (Settings → Automation) and ask anything, typed or by voice: what's from Burgundy, what did we drink this year, move that bottle to rack B. Every consequential change asks first, and nothing is ever deleted.

Getting help

Email bill@1041soft.com. Include your device and OS version, and — if a label was misread — the producer and vintage you expected. Recognition problems are much easier to fix with a concrete example.

Common questions

A label was recognised incorrectly. Suggestions stay untrusted until you confirm them. Edit any field before confirming, or enter a wine manually.

Does it work offline? Capture and on-device text recognition work without a connection. Structured identification runs when connectivity returns.

Do my photographs sync? The Mac, iPad, and iPhone apps stay in step through your private iCloud database, and every camera shot is also saved to your own Photos library in an "Oenora" album.

A bottle was confirmed with the wrong identity. Open it in the Cellar and edit the wine identity directly — or tap "Re-read the photographs" to run it through the current recognizer again. Corrections are logged; the photographs are never altered.
