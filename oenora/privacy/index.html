Oenora Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

Oenora records the wine you own. Label photographs are written to disk before any recognition runs, and your cellar is a versioned, restart-safe record on your own device.

Camera and photo library

Camera access is used to photograph wine labels. Photo library access is used to select existing label photographs. Both are used only for that purpose.

Analytics and tracking

Oenora contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
