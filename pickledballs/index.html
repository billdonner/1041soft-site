On Court: fair rounds, every time

Anyone can run a game. Open your club and tap Start games, or pick whoever showed up. The Companion builds the pairings so nobody partners twice until everyone has, and extras rotate fairly. Run the rounds, start the built-in timer, and enter scores as you go. Close the app mid-session and pick up exactly where you left off.

Listen: see your court live

Players tap Listen, and their court and score show up on their own phone as the game unfolds, over the Wi‑Fi in the room. No more crowding around one screen between rounds.

Your club, free

Your roster is your club. Name it and add your players; it gets its own tab, and every game starts from it. Share it with a link so your other organizers keep the same list on their phones: names and ratings only, never phone numbers. Free forever.

Pickleball, tennis, and ping pong — singles or doubles, your choice per game.

No account. Your players and games stay on your device. The court scheduler is on-device math: no AI, no cloud. See how fair courts work.
