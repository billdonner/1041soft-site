Support

PickledBalls turns a pile of players into fair, balanced courts in seconds — for pickleball, tennis, and ping pong pickup games.

Getting started

  • Tap New Game, pick your sport, and set the number of courts
  • Add players from your roster (or type new names on the spot)
  • PickledBalls generates balanced rounds so everyone plays with and against different partners
  • Record scores as you go — past games live under Recent Games

For the details of how rounds are generated, see How Scheduling Works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
No. PickledBalls works completely anonymously — no sign-in, no email required.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Your roster, games, and scores are stored on your device. No internet connection is needed to schedule or run a session.

Which sports are supported?
Pickleball, tennis, and ping pong.

How does the pairing work?
Rounds are built so partners and opponents rotate fairly — nobody sits twice before everyone sits once, and repeat pairings are minimized. Full explanation here.

Where is my data stored?
Everything you create stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
