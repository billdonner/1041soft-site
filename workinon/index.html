workinOn

An AI-powered focus list and status board for iPhone and Apple Watch.

User Guide Shortcuts Support Privacy Developer API

What it does

Tell workinOn what you're working on — naturally, in your own words — and it manages the list. Your home screen widget and Apple Watch always show what matters, and other apps can post live status updates that pin to the top with color-coded severity.

Highlights

  • Natural-language list management powered by Apple Intelligence (on-device)
  • Large home-screen widget with photo thumbnails
  • Apple Watch companion app — glanceable list with four complication families
  • Status item severity — tap to mark items info (blue), warning (yellow), or urgent (red); watch complication tints accordingly
  • Multiple named lists — switch on iPhone or independently on the watch
  • Native Shortcuts and Siri integration — "Hey Siri, add to workinOn"
  • Share Sheet support — "Add to workinOn" from any app
  • Status-board URL scheme for app-to-app integration
